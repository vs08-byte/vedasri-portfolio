ABOUT ME

A curious learner who learns by doing.

I’m Vedasri, a curious learner and Intermediate MPC student from Hyderabad. Alongside my studies, I have been managing my mother’s Meeseva business since 2021, gaining practical experience by handling the day-to-day work independently.

My curiosity has also led me toward AI tools, technology, video editing, and content creation. I enjoy learning by exploring things myself, experimenting, researching, and turning what I learn into something useful. I use tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude as part of my learning and experimentation.

In April 2026, I started Online Muchatlu, an independent Telugu-language information page created from my own experience of feeling confused about which path to choose for the future. Through the page, I create and explain information for students, freshers, graduates, job seekers, and anyone curious to learn—covering career paths, job updates, and useful information in a simple and understandable way. I handle the complete process myself, from research and scripting to recording, editing, and publishing.

I’m still learning and exploring, and I don’t want to present myself as an expert. My goal is to keep learning, build meaningful things, grow through experience, and eventually build a successful business.
